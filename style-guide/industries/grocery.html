🛒 Grocery Industry Themes

Optimized configurations for grocery stores, supermarkets, and food retailers. These themes emphasize fresh deals, promotional pricing, and loyalty programs.

🎯 Why These Choices Work for Grocery

Industry-Specific Requirements

Grocery circulars need to communicate time-sensitive promotions, loyalty pricing, and digital coupons clearly. Customers scan quickly for deals on perishables, so bold pricing and clear value propositions are essential. The themes below are optimized for high-volume promotional content with emphasis on freshness and savings.

🏷️ Price Sticker Strategy

  • Circular shapes (50% radius) for traditional feel
  • Bold Oswald font for price legibility
  • High contrast red/white combinations
  • Multi-buy deals (2 for $5, 3/$10)

💰 Deal Type Focus

  • NumFor - Volume discounts (2 for $5)
  • BOGO - Customer acquisition
  • SaveX - Clear savings message
  • Loyalty pricing - Member exclusives

🎨 Color Psychology

  • Red - Creates urgency and appetite
  • Yellow - Highlights savings/value
  • Green - Fresh produce association
  • Orange - Energy and freshness

✅ Recommended Configurations

Best Practices for Grocery Circulars

Price Tag Style: Use circular stickers (50% radius) for traditional grocery feel, or horizontal/rectangle shapes for modern, space-efficient layouts.

Digital Coupons: Yellow (#FFCF16) for coupon backgrounds with high contrast. Red alt coupons (#EB0000) for special promotions.

Loyalty Programs: Use teal/cyan (#007B8A) for member-exclusive pricing to differentiate from standard promotional pricing.

Font Choices: Oswald for prices (bold, condensed, high legibility), Open Sans or Montserrat for body text (clean, professional).
